# My project brief
Which residential areas in Shomolu LGA are more than 2 km from the nearest emergency healthcare facility?

## Why it matters
Knowing which residential areas in Shomolu LGA are more than 2 km from the nearest emergency healthcare facility can help identify areas with limited access to emergency medical services. This information can support better healthcare planning by showing where additional facilities or improved emergency access may be needed.

## DATASET 
- Ward boundaries - GRID3
- Road Network - OSM via QuickOSM
- Healthcare facilities - QuickOSM/Google earth pro
- residential areas - OSM via QuickOSM
